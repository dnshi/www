# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are recruiters, engineering peers, collaborators, and people who search for Dean Shi by name. They want a quick read on seniority, taste, technical range, and how to make contact.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal web presence for Dean Shi. It should communicate engineering depth, product taste, and a clear career arc without feeling like a dated resume template. Success means a visitor can understand who Dean is, where he has worked, what he is strong at, and how to reach him in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, kinetic, and personal. The site should feel like a compact command center for a senior web engineer: precise enough for credibility, expressive enough to be remembered.

## My Path

The experience is now branded around Dean, not a fictional software product.
The path runs chronologically from Acadia and early product teams through
Amazon / Microsoft, Amplitude, and Microsoft Fabric. Each stop must communicate
its dates, companies, capability shift, and specific work without opening a
modal. Details are optional depth, never the only source of personal meaning.
The scene must express that work: progression from code to product, delivery
with a worker branch, four products feeding one platform, and three Fabric
capabilities sharing a foundation. Do not invent personal beliefs.

The primary experience is a full-screen, relaxed exploration game, not a landing
page with a decorative model. Visitors pilot a luminous signal along a winding
river through four spatially distinct career worlds. Mouse movement controls
drift; wheel, touch swipes, arrow keys, and explicit travel buttons navigate.
There are no collisions, timers, failure states, or required dexterity.
Arrival automatically marks a chapter explored; reading details is not a game
objective or a prerequisite for understanding the career.

Use depth, architectural scale, luminous trails, bloom, and activation feedback
to make exploration feel alive. Each landmark expresses real experience before
any detail panel is opened.
Keep the HUD restrained and readable: body copy is at least 18px, with much
larger world titles. Avoid microtype dashboards and long-page portfolio layouts.

Provide immediate access to the complete resume and a world selector. Preserve
all resume facts in HTML, direct chapter links, keyboard operation, touch
controls, reduced-motion travel without camera animation, and a complete
non-WebGL fallback. Capabilities link to evidence rather than percentage scores.
Never invent achievements or turn game metaphors into claims about real projects.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, beige editorial portfolios, purple gradient templates, overdecorated glass cards, and the old Semantic UI resume look. Avoid fake case studies or inflated claims not present in the source data.

## Design Principles

Show the signal first: name, role, location, contact, and career weight should be immediately visible.

Make engineering feel tactile: use layout, rhythm, and motion to suggest systems thinking without relying on terminal cliches alone.

Keep the page scannable: every section should support quick comparison and mobile reading.

Preserve factual restraint: personality comes from design, not embellished resume copy.

Design for return visits: the site should feel polished enough to represent long-term craft, not a throwaway landing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced motion preferences. Keep keyboard focus visible, links descriptive, and layout readable from narrow mobile screens through wide desktop displays.
