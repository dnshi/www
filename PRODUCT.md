# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are recruiters, engineering peers, collaborators, and people who search for Dean Shi by name. They want a quick read on seniority, taste, technical range, and how to make contact.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal web presence for Dean Shi. It should communicate engineering depth, product taste, and a clear career arc without feeling like a dated resume template. Success means a visitor can understand who Dean is, where he has worked, what he is strong at, and how to reach him in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, kinetic, and personal. The site should feel like a compact command center for a senior web engineer: precise enough for credibility, expressive enough to be remembered.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, beige editorial portfolios, purple gradient templates, overdecorated glass cards, and the old Semantic UI resume look. Avoid fake case studies or inflated claims not present in the source data.

## Design Principles

Show the signal first: name, role, location, contact, and career weight should be immediately visible.

Make engineering feel tactile: use layout, rhythm, and motion to suggest systems thinking without relying on terminal cliches alone.

Keep the page scannable: every section should support quick comparison and mobile reading.

Preserve factual restraint: personality comes from design, not embellished resume copy.

Design for return visits: the site should feel polished enough to represent long-term craft, not a throwaway landing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced motion preferences. Keep keyboard focus visible, links descriptive, and layout readable from narrow mobile screens through wide desktop displays.
